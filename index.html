Rapid safety checklist

  • Confirm patient and current weight.
  • Confirm drug, indication, dose, route, and product concentration.
  • Use current hospital emergency / CPR protocols.
  • Arrange an independent double-check for high-risk medications.
  • Reassess and document the response.

Global medication concentrations

A concentration is shared wherever that medication appears. Changing concentration recalculates volume only; it does not change the dose range. Settings are saved in this browser on this device.

About default values

Defaults are editable starting values and may not match the products stocked at your hospital. Verify every concentration and unit against the actual product label before use. Local browser storage is not encrypted medical-record storage.
